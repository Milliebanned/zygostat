01 / Verdict

The case for NEAR, weighed on chain.

NEAR's long-term value now rests on NEAR Intents becoming the place cross-chain money settles. This thesis tests that on three things: is Intents used, does NEAR capture part of what it earns, and does the contract holding users' money actually hold it. Four conditions, set in advance, decide whether the case stands.

03 / The exploit

The October exploit, read from chain state.

On 1 October 2026 NEAR Intents was exploited for about $3.8M. The official postmortem says the contract recorded deposits that the token contract had refunded, so its ledger showed more than it held. That is a statement about two numbers on a public chain, so it can be checked.

The two lines should sit on top of each other. From 2026-09-30 00:44 UTC to 2026-10-02 20:00 UTC they do not: at the worst reading users were credited 3,842,954 tokens while the contract held 5,805, a gap of $3,834,778. The gap closes by 2026-10-02 20:30 UTC.

The two calls behind this chart (paste to reproduce the peak reading)
# 1. Credited to users: what intents.near's ledger says exists of this token
curl -s https://archival-rpc.mainnet.fastnear.com -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"query","params":{"request_type":"call_function","account_id":"intents.near","method_name":"mt_batch_supply","args_base64":"eyJ0b2tlbl9pZHMiOlsibmVwMjQ1OnYyXzEub21uaS5ob3QudGc6NTZfMkNNTXlWVEdaa2V5TlpUU3ZTNXNhcnpmaXI2ZyJdfQ==","block_id":217996962}}'

# 2. Held: what the token contract says intents.near owns
curl -s https://archival-rpc.mainnet.fastnear.com -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"query","params":{"request_type":"call_function","account_id":"v2_1.omni.hot.tg","method_name":"mt_batch_balance_of","args_base64":"eyJhY2NvdW50X2lkIjoiaW50ZW50cy5uZWFyIiwidG9rZW5faWRzIjpbIjU2XzJDTU15VlRHWmtleU5aVFN2UzVzYXJ6ZmlyNmciXX0=","block_id":217996962}}'

04 / History

A year of readings.

The same check at UTC midnight blocks: weekly from 2025-11-16, daily from 20 September 2026. Each reading compares credits with holdings for every multi-token balance and for the largest fungible tokens.

Apart from the October exploit, no reading shows a material shortfall. Since 2026-10-08, 0 of 41 readings show a material shortfall.

06 / Risk model

Pricing the risk.

One exploited loss in 1.93 years of operation gives a rate of 0.52 events a year, with a 90% interval from 0.03 to 2.46. The loss took 1.87% of custody. Every event so far was fully recovered, but one recovery is not a rule. Move the inputs and watch what the next event would cost.

Formula
gross loss per event = f * C
net cost per event   = gross loss * (1 - recovered) * h + D
expected cost a year = events a year * net cost per event

R = captured revenue a year      C = custody (credits, USD)     f = share of custody lost
D = revenue lost to one pause    h = share of an unrecovered loss paid from captured revenue

07 / Method

What would change the thesis.

Four conditions, each a number recomputed on every build. The thresholds were written on 2026-11-13 after a first look at the data, so today they describe the past. They bind on every later rebuild.

ConditionRuleNowStatus
K1 SolvencyNo reading after 2026-10-08 shows a material shortfall (at least $100 and 1 basis point of a token's credits).0 of 41 readings✓Holds
K2 UsageIntents volume over the last 30 days is not below the same 30 days a year earlier.+39%✓Holds
K3 Value captureRevenue captured by NEAR (90 days, annualised) is at least 10% of the dollar value of one year of new NEAR supply.22.4%✓Holds
K4 Security costExpected yearly cost of loss events (observed rate, half recovered) is at most 25% of captured revenue.4.8%✓Holds

Limits

  • The check covers the 203 tokens on the NEAR Intents 1Click list that have a token contract on NEAR. Another 28 listed assets have none and cannot be checked this way. Tokens held by the contract but not on the list, and NFTs, are not checked.
  • It proves the NEAR side only: that intents.near holds the NEAR-side tokens it credits. It does not prove that each bridged token is backed on its origin chain.
  • Confidential balances are not visible on the public ledger and are outside this check.
  • Historical readings are spaced (weekly, daily, or 30 minutes apart). A shortfall that opened and closed between two readings would not appear.
  • Dollar values use one price per token, taken on 2026-11-16. Token amounts are exact; the dollar totals for past dates are approximate.
  • Volume, fees, revenue, supply and activity are quoted from DefiLlama and NearBlocks, not recomputed.
  • The event rate rests on a single exploited loss. The interval around it is wide, and the model shows that width instead of hiding it.

Every number in this product is produced by node src/build.mjs from the raw data committed in data/raw/. The full argument is in THESIS.md. Built at NEAR HACKATHON, Investment Thesis track. Research, not financial advice.
